Features

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
IN ONE DASHBOARD

All features work over your local Wi-Fi network — no internet required, no data leaves your home.

🔗

Quick Links

Open your favourite websites on the PC with a single tap from your phone.

🚀

Launch Apps

Start Telegram, VS Code, Notepad or any custom app instantly.

🔊

Volume Control

Raise, lower or mute your PC volume in 10% increments from anywhere.

📸

Screenshot

Capture your screen and view it live on your phone in one tap.

📊

System Monitor

Real-time CPU, RAM and Disk stats with colour-coded progress bars.

📁

File Explorer

Browse your Downloads, Desktop and Documents — download any file straight to your phone.

⚙️

Process Manager

View top 10 memory-hungry processes and kill any of them with one tap.

🔒

System Controls

Lock, Sleep, Restart or Shutdown your PC remotely with confirmation dialogs.

🖥️

System Tray

Runs silently in the background. Right-click the tray icon to open or exit.

Setup

UP AND RUNNING
IN 4 STEPS

No cloud accounts, no subscriptions. Just download and run.

01

Download

Grab the latest LuminaRemote.exe from the releases page. No Python required.

02

Run

Double-click the exe. A tray icon appears and your browser opens automatically.

LuminaRemote.exe
03

Find your IP

Open CMD and run ipconfig. Note your IPv4 address.

ipconfig
04

Connect

Open your phone's browser and navigate to your PC's IP on port 5000.

192.168.x.x:5000
